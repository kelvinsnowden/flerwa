## Caveats on source quality

**[REC] Treat this as a well-researched starting point, not verified market intelligence.**

- **The single most important source — The Flip's Lynk post-mortem — could not be fetched directly** (blocked by the network egress proxy). Its findings here come from search excerpts and corroborating coverage. **Read the original before finalising strategy; it is the closest thing to a map of this exact minefield.**
- **Competitor scale is unverified.** I could not establish transaction volume, funding or team size for My-Fundi, Balozy, Kalinoi or Juakali Hub. Their public presence suggests early stage, but **sign up and transact on each before finalising positioning** — desk research cannot distinguish a real marketplace from a directory with a booking form.
- **The diaspora willingness-to-pay assumption is unverified by any source.** It is the load-bearing assumption of the recommended strategy and the reason `11` opens with a 30-day validation and a written kill criterion.
- **All Kenyan prices in this document are placeholders.** None is market data.
- **Unit-cost inputs are estimates** and must be replaced with real aggregator and staffing quotes.
- **Nothing here is legal, tax or financial advice.** Items marked **[LEGAL — COUNSEL REQUIRED]** are a briefing for a Kenyan advocate, not a substitute for one.
