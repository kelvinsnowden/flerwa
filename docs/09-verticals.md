## 1. Remote-Principal Services — **the recommended wedge**

**What it is.** Being someone's verified eyes, hands and judgement in Kenya when they cannot be there: property inspection before a deposit, viewing on their behalf, quarterly checks on a rental, supervising a fundi, collecting documents, verifying goods before payment, attending to a family matter.

**Why it wins on the criteria that matter:**
- **[FACT]** The trust deficit is documented and worsening — housing scams surging in Kilimani, Kileleshwa, Jamhuri and Lavington, exploiting the deposit convention; diaspora investors targeted **specifically because distance makes due diligence nearly impossible**; press guidance concluding that a *"trusted, verifiable representative in Kenya"* is among the most effective protections available.
- **[FACT]** The buyer population is large and demonstrably moves money: **US$5.04B remitted in 2025**, CBK projecting **US$5.24B for 2026**.
- **Leakage resistance is structural**, not engineered: the customer has no relationship to fall back on. The platform is the trust.
- **Completion is verifiable remotely** — the deliverable *is* evidence, so escrow and dispute resolution work cleanly.
- **Supply is largely fungible** across job types, which is the escape from "every category is a different company."

**The honest risks:**
- **[ASSUMPTION] Market size unverified.** No source sizes willingness to pay. **This is the first thing to test and it is cheap to test.**
- **Agency fraud is the existential risk** (`06`). A bribed inspector is worse than no inspector.
- Low frequency outside the property-owning segment.
- **[LEGAL — COUNSEL REQUIRED]** Property representation, and the boundary between reporting facts and giving professional opinion on title or valuation.

**[REC] Launch product: "Know Before You Pay" — KSh 6,000.** Geotagged photo set, video walkthrough, 12-point structured checklist, delivered within 48 hours. **[ASSUMPTION] Price to be validated in week one.**

## 3. High-Value Fundi Work — **Vertical 3**

Repairs, installation, painting, moving, appliance and electrical work. **Only jobs above KSh 5,000.**

**Why it is genuinely attractive:** the trust deficit is severe and specific — overcharging, botched work, and above all the **materials advance** that disappears. **[REC] The materials escrow in `04` and `07` is a real, buildable differentiator that no competitor models.** Episodic use means moderate leakage resistance. And it is the natural adjacency from property: the customer who just had a flat inspected needs it repaired.

**Why it is not first:** **[FACT]** Lynk's vetting and training costs forced it to build an academy, and its vocational partnerships failed. Competence assessment for trades is slow, expensive and does not transfer. Quality variance is high and quality control requires physical inspection. **[FACT]** Urban Company's lesson is that this vertical works *managed*, not as a light marketplace — which is a capital-intensive commitment you should make only with evidence.

**[REC] Enter with a narrow, productised set** — Fix My Leak, Paint My 1-Bed, Move My Flat — not "browse fundis." Fixed prices, defined inclusions, workmanship guarantee.

## 4. Property Services — **not a separate vertical; the spine of Vertical 1**

Your brief §7 asks me to think hard about property and to avoid becoming a listings site. **[REC] Agreed, and the conclusion is stronger than "add property services":**

**Property is not a category alongside the others. It is the demand engine for the entire wedge.** The property lifecycle generates the whole service catalogue:

```
Considering a purchase/rental   → inspection, viewing, title-adjacent verification
Committing                      → document collection, agent verification
Moving in                       → cleaning, repairs, furniture assembly, internet setup
Owning (recurring)              → quarterly checks, tenant vetting, rent oversight,
                                  maintenance supervision, caretaker verification
Selling / letting               → photography, listing content, viewings on your behalf
```

**[REC] Do not build listings, do not hold deposits, do not take a cut of rent or sale.** All three drag you into estate-agency and payments regulation, and the second is explicitly ruled out in `06`. **Sell the human service required to navigate the asset** — which is exactly the framing in your brief, and it is correct.

**[REC] The landlord conversion is the most valuable transition in the business model:** a one-off inspection customer who buys the property becomes a **recurring quarterly customer**, converting the wedge's weakest attribute (frequency) into its strongest.

Cleaning, mama fua, laundry, gardening, errands, small deliveries.

**[REC] Excluded from the plan entirely, for now.** Below the KSh 5,000 line; highest leakage of any category; **[FACT]** the categories on which Lynk and SweepSouth both foundered in this exact city; and for domestic work specifically, **[FACT]** a gazetted minimum wage of KSh 18,047/month and over two million informally employed workers create **[LEGAL — COUNSEL REQUIRED]** employment-classification exposure quite unlike booking a tradesperson.

**[REC] The only conditions under which these open later:** per-order human cost has been engineered to near zero, basket size has been raised through bundling, and you are prepared to run them **managed** rather than as a listing. That is Urban Company's model and it is a different company with a different balance sheet.

## Final launch recommendation (Brief §60)

> ### Launch with Remote-Principal Services, in the Nairobi property corridor, with property verification as the entry product.
> ### Add Business & Creator Services in month 6. Add high-value fundi work in month 12.
> ### Never add cleaning, mama fua or errands unless the economics are re-proven from scratch.

**Why this ordering and not the reverse:**

1. It is the **only** vertical that scores well on both triple-weighted criteria.
2. It is the only one where **the trust deficit is documented in the press rather than assumed by you**.
3. Its buyer earns hard currency and already sends money blind.
4. Its supply is the cheapest to verify — integrity and documentation, not a trade skill.
5. It generates the natural adjacency into fundi work (Vertical 3) and landlord recurrence.
6. **Nobody is serving it.** Every competitor found — Lynk, SweepSouth, My-Fundi, Balozy, Kalinoi, Juakali Hub — is building for the resident who is standing in their own home.
